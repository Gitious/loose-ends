LOOSE ENDS — 3-MINUTE DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
==========================================

Title card: "Loose Ends — the agent that ties them up"

Total runtime: 3:00
Voiceover: ~420 words, natural delivery


===========================================
ACT 1 — THE PAIN (0:00–0:12)
===========================================

VISUAL: Fast cuts on a rising tone.
  - Gmail inbox scrolling fast (847 unread)
  - Slack sidebar with red unread badges on 6 channels
  - GitHub notifications tab: "52 unread"
  - Calendar view with overlapping blocks

VOICEOVER (0:00–0:08):
"Every day, you drop threads. An email you meant to reply to.
A PR that went stale. A Slack DM from Tuesday. Your tools don't
talk to each other — so the threads just pile up."

VISUAL (0:08–0:12): Hard cut to black.
  White text types on screen: "Loose Ends."

VOICEOVER:
"We built an agent that picks them up."


===========================================
ACT 2 — THE REVEAL (0:12–0:22)
===========================================

VISUAL: Dashboard fades in — prioritized feed of cards
(not a CRUD table). Urgency tiers color-coded (red/yellow/green).
Agent Suggestions tray visible at top.

VOICEOVER:
"Loose Ends connects to Gmail, Calendar, GitHub, and Slack through
Auth0 Token Vault — so your credentials never touch our servers.
Then it reads everything together. And decides what to do."

VISUAL: Camera pushes in on one suggestion card.
  Card reads: "Book meeting with Sarah — you're free at 1pm Thursday"


===========================================
ACT 3 — THE HERO FLOW: BOOK & REPLY (0:22–1:42)
===========================================

0:22–0:35 | VISUAL: Full screen, actual email from Sarah.
  "Hey — any chance for coffee Thursday 1pm?
   Want to talk about the Q2 roadmap."

VOICEOVER:
"Here's a real email. Someone wants thirty minutes of your Thursday."

---

0:35–0:52 | VISUAL: Split screen — email left, agent reasoning right,
  typing in real time:

    Reading email from sarah.chen@…
    Extracting: meeting request, Thursday, 1pm, 30min
    Checking calendar Thursday 12:30–2:00… you're free.
    Checking FGA: calendar.can_create ✓  gmail.can_reply ✓
    Drafting reply in your voice.

VOICEOVER:
"It reads the email. Checks the calendar. Checks your permissions
through Auth0 FGA — because not every contact should be
auto-scheduled. Then it drafts the reply in your voice."

---

0:52–1:10 | ★★★ THE HERO SHOT ★★★
  (shoot this 10 times, pick the best one)

VISUAL: Physical desk. Laptop + phone both in frame.
  Phone buzzes. CIBA push appears on phone screen:

    "Loose Ends: Book 'Coffee with Sarah' Thursday 1pm
     AND send confirmation reply? Approve / Deny"

  Thumb enters frame. Taps Approve. Face ID flash.

VOICEOVER:
"Loose Ends doesn't just ask your browser. It asks YOU.
Auth0 CIBA pushes the approval to your phone. Because an agent
that can send email on your behalf should need more than a cookie."

---

1:10–1:25 | VISUAL: Hold the two-screen shot, no cuts.
  On laptop, in real time:
    - Google Calendar opens
    - "Coffee with Sarah" event materializes Thursday 1pm
  Window switches to second Gmail account (Sarah's):
    - New email arrives: "Thursday 1pm works — sending invite"

VOICEOVER:
"Event created. Reply sent. One approval. Two services.
Zero context-switching."

---

1:25–1:42 | VISUAL: Agent card updates to green checkmark.
  Sub-text: "Token Vault + FGA + CIBA — all in one flow."

VOICEOVER:
"That's Token Vault, FGA, and CIBA — all three Auth0 primitives —
in one flow. But that was one thread. You have eleven."

===========================================

VOICEOVER:
"Flip the switch. Now the agent initiates."

---

1:50–2:15 | ★ PHONE-FIRST SHOT — SINGLE UNBROKEN TAKE ★
  Notifications stack 2 seconds apart, thumb taps each:

    • "Reply to Marcus about design review?"    → Approve
    • "Decline Friday 4pm — conflicts with standup?" → Approve
    • "Nudge reviewers on PR #284 (stale 6 days)?"   → Approve

VOICEOVER:
"The agent finds the threads. You approve on your phone.
One tap each. No laptop open. No tabs. Every action goes through
CIBA — so nothing happens without you."

2:15–2:22 | VISUAL: Cut to laptop. Activity log shows
  four green checkmarks stacked.

VOICEOVER:

===========================================
PACING NOTES
===========================================

- The 0:52–1:10 hero shot is the entire demo. Everything before
  builds to it, everything after rhymes with it. Shoot it like
  a film, not a screen recording.

- Cut on the beat. The opening four cuts should land on a rising
  tone that resolves when "Loose Ends" appears on black.

- The auto-act sequence MUST be a single unbroken take on the
  phone. No jump cuts. Judges need to feel the rhythm of
  tap-tap-tap-tap = done.

- Resist the urge to show the settings page, the connected
  accounts page, or any Auth0 infrastructure UI. The infrastructure
  should be INVISIBLE in the demo and only named in the voiceover.

- Keep agent reasoning text legible but DON'T pause for it.
  The agent is confident. It moves fast. That's the personality.
